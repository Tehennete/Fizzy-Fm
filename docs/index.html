FIZZY-FM

FM-1 (MIDI: USB, DIN or Bluetooth)

Wavetables of your own (USER 1 to 3): a WAV of 2048-sample frames, or one cycle

Fizzy-Fm is free software (GPL-3.0). This page is the synth's own code in WebAssembly: what you hear and the screen are what the FM-1 does, but for its converter and its speaker. Nothing leaves your computer.
