A firmware built by `build.sh` (`aplat-X.Y.fwsc`) is fm1-dock with APLAT in it: it carries fm1-dock's and its
platform's code and their copyright lines, under the same GPL-3.0-only.

The filter is the well-known state-variable filter with trapezoidal integrators (as published by Andrew Simper,
Cytomic, in "Linear Trapezoidal Integrated SVF"), written here from its equations in fixed point.

APLAT shares no code, wavetable, sound or artwork with any other wavetable synthesizer.
